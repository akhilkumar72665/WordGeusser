Tumhara game concept kaafi interesting hai. Isme **Wordle + Word Guessing + Multiplayer + Strategy** ka combination ban sakta hai. Isko ek proper multiplayer party game banaya ja sakta hai, jisme 5–6 se lekar 10–15 players tak khel saken.

Main kuch ideas deta hoon jo tumhare existing concept ko aur interesting, competitive aur strategic bana sakte hain.

# 1. Game ke Basic Rules ko Improve Karna

### A. Game Setup
Game start hone se pehle sabhi players kuch settings decide karenge:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–15 |
| Word Length | 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10+ |
| Difficulty | Easy, Medium, Hard |
| Language | English, Hindi |
| Turn Order | Clockwise, Anti-clockwise, Random |
| Guess Limit | 2, 3, 5, 7, Unlimited |
| Game Mode | Elimination, Points |
| Rounds | 3, 5, 10, Custom |

### B. Secret Word Selection
- Har player apna ek secret word select karega.
- Kisi doosre player ko word nahi pata hona chahiye.
- Sabhi words selected difficulty aur alphabet length ke according hone chahiye.
- Duplicate words ko prevent kiya ja sakta hai.

### C. Letter Guessing
Example:

6 players hain:

1. Player A
2. Player B
3. Player C
4. Player D
5. Player E
6. Player F

Player A, Player B se poochta hai:

**"Kya tumhare word mein A hai?"**

Agar hai:
- Player B batayega ki A kis position par hai.
- Example: Position 2 aur 5.

Agar nahi:
- Player B bolega No.
- Turn next player ko transfer hoga.

### D. Word Guessing
Koi bhi player kisi bhi opponent ka complete word guess kar sakta hai.

Example:

Player A: "Tumhara word APPLE hai?"

Agar correct:
- Player B eliminate ho jayega.
- Player A ko points milenge.

Agar incorrect:
- Player A ka guess attempt consume hoga.
- Game continue hoga.

# 5. Ek Important Rule Jo Add Karna Chahiye (optional in game rule)

**Word Reveal Protection**

Maan lo Player A ne Player B ka word guess kar liya.

Ab Player B eliminate ho gaya.

Lekin Player B ka word sabhi players ko reveal nahi hona chahiye.

Kyunki agar word reveal ho gaya toh baaki players ko advantage mil jayega.

Isliye:
- Word sirf guess karne wale ko confirmation mile.
- Baaki players ko word reveal na ho.
- Eliminated player ke baad game continue ho.

# 7. Meri Sabse Important Recommendations

Tumhare original concept mein 3 cheezein bahut carefully design karni hongi:

**1. Guessing Turn vs Letter Asking Turn**

Dono ko separate actions banana chahiye. Player apni turn par alphabet pooch sakta hai ya kisi opponent ka complete word guess kar sakta hai.

**2. Guess Attempts**

Har player ko individual guess limit dena better rahega. Example: 5 incorrect word guesses ke baad us player ka guessing option lock ho jaye, lekin wo alphabet questions pooch sakta hai.

**3. Elimination aur Points**

Elimination aur points ko separate rakhna chahiye. Kisi player ka word guess hone par wo eliminate ho, lekin uske already earned points leaderboard mein rahen.

## 8. Ek Unique Feature: Mind Reading Challenge (optional in game rule)

Isko game ka special feature bana sakte hain.

Maan lo 6 players hain aur sabke words 7 letters ke hain.

Player A ko Player B ke word mein 3 letters pata chal chuke hain.

Ab Player A ko decide karna hai:

- Aur letters discover kare?
- Ya directly complete word guess kare?

Jitni jaldi correct guess karega, utne zyada points milenge.

Yahan risk vs reward ka element aayega.

- Players kisi ko bhi opponent ko select karte hain. But kisi ek aadmi ke do opponent nhi honge

- player ka valid English word ho.

- Selected length ke according word hona chahiye.
- Difficulty selected level ke according honi chahiye.
- Proper nouns, names, abbreviations aur invalid spellings default mein allowed nahi honi chahiye.

- Word select karne ke baad game start hone tak change nahi kar sakte.
- Kisi bhi player ko doosre players ke words nahi dikhne chahiye.
